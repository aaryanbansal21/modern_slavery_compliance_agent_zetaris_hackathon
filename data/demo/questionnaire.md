# Meridian Property Group: Supplier Modern Slavery Questionnaire 2026

Supplier: Harbourside Facility Services Pty Ltd
Requested by: Meridian Property Group, Procurement and Sustainability (demo client)
Due: within 10 business days

Meridian is a reporting entity under the Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth). Please answer each question and attach evidence where you can.

1. Does your organisation have a policy or supplier code of conduct that addresses modern slavery, forced labour or child labour?
2. List the countries where the goods and materials you supply to us, or use to deliver services to us, are produced.
3. Do you buy any goods that are known to carry a high risk of forced labour or child labour, for example gloves, textiles, garments, electronics or solar products? Give details.
4. Have you screened your suppliers against Australian sanctions lists? What were the results?
5. Do you use labour hire or subcontractors to deliver services to Meridian? How do you check the working conditions of those workers?
6. What due diligence have you carried out on your suppliers in the last 12 months, such as questionnaires, audits or certifications?
7. Have staff responsible for procurement or recruitment received training on modern slavery?
8. Do you have a grievance or whistleblowing mechanism that workers in your operations can use?
9. Have you identified any modern slavery risks or incidents in your operations or supply chain? What actions have you taken or planned?
10. Will you notify Meridian if you identify modern slavery in your operations or supply chain that relates to the services you provide to us?
